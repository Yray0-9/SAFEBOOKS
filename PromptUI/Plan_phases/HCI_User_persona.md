### Accessibility Condition

**Low Vision — Diabetic Retinopathy**

Elena was diagnosed with Type 2 Diabetes at age 42. By 47, her vision started getting blurry. She now has partial sight — she can see shapes and large text, but small fonts, thin lines, and light-colored text are hard for her to read. Her left eye is worse than her right. She gets regular eye treatment but the damage is not fully reversible.

---

### Assistive Tools She Uses

- Reading glasses with magnification
- Browser zoom set to 120–150%
- Desk lamp with bright daylight bulb

---

### Goals

- Keep working as a bookkeeper despite her vision loss
- Use a system with big, clear text she can read without straining
- Avoid number entry mistakes from misreading digits on screen
- Stay independent — doesn't want to ask others to read for her

---

### Frustrations

- Text is too small on most websites and accounting tools
- Gray text on white backgrounds is almost invisible to her
- Financial tables with tightly packed rows — she loses track easily
- Tiny buttons without labels — hard to see and easy to misclick
- Pop-up messages that disappear too fast before she can read them
- Gets logged out when she takes too long verifying her entries

---

### What She Needs from a System

- Font size at least 16px, readable without extreme zooming
- Strong color contrast between text and background
- Table rows with alternating colors so she can follow across
- Buttons big enough to click with text labels, not just icons
- Error and success messages that stay visible until dismissed
- Longer session timeout so she doesn't lose unsaved work

---

### Daily Scenario

She opens her laptop in the morning, zooms Chrome to 175%, and logs into her bookkeeping system. She enters receipts for her three small business clients, double-checks every number by zooming in, then prints reports in the afternoon. By 2 PM her eyes are tired so she switches to keyboard-only navigation. She reviews printed reports with a magnifying glass before handing them to clients.

---

### Quote

> "My mind is still sharp — I've done this job for 25 years. I just need the screen to cooperate with my eyes. Bigger text and clearer colors, that's all I'm asking."
