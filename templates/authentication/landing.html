Frequently Asked Questions

To protect client records, SafeBooks enforces a strict onboarding verification. After signing up, verify your email and submit proof of bookkeeping qualifications or experience. An administrator reviews your application before you can manage client records.

Your bookkeeper account provides access to the client profiles and financial records you manage. You can also enable the Client Details Lock in Settings to require password confirmation before opening a client profile.

You can print financial summaries, record status reports, and client status reports from Reports. Open a client’s Financial Records to print their worksheet. Use Print / Save PDF to save a copy.
