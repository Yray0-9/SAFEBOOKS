Frequently Asked Questions

Learn more about onboarding, administrative review, and data privacy safeguards.

To protect client records, SafeBooks enforces a strict onboarding verification. After signing up and confirming your email address, your account status is set to Pending Approval. The Guild administration console manually reviews each application. Once verified, you will be active and can immediately manage clients.

SafeBooks implements strict schema-level data separation. Every client and tax transaction is scoped exclusively under the bookkeeper who registered them. System administrators can manage and review bookkeeper account active states, but they have no technical access to view client profiles or financial transactions.

Bookkeepers can instantly generate and print audit-ready compliance summaries, risk reports, and monthly financial ledger reports. Export formats include standardized printable PDF worksheets and spreadsheets to match standard bookkeeping documentation procedures.
