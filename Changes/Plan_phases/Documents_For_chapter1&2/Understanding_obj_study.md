# Understanding the Forecasting Objective of SAFEBOOKS

The forecasting component of SAFEBOOKS should be reflected in the objectives as a system functionality rather than merely as a dashboard visualization.

The objective should describe what the forecasting module provides to users, such as generating projected sales, expenses, taxes, and net income based on historical financial records. It should also highlight that the forecasting model used by the system is selected through the evaluation of multiple forecasting algorithms to ensure reliable forecasting results.

The purpose of comparing forecasting algorithms is not necessarily to make all algorithms visible to users. Instead, the comparison serves as part of the study's methodology to identify the forecasting approach that produces the most accurate predictions. Once the most accurate forecasting algorithm is identified, that algorithm becomes the forecasting engine used by the system.

Therefore, the forecasting objective should focus on the implemented functionality of the system, such as generating financial forecasts, presenting forecast reports, displaying forecast visualizations, and supporting financial planning through predictive insights. The algorithm comparison supports the development of this functionality but does not necessarily need to appear as a separate user-facing feature.

The forecasting module may include:

* Forecasted Sales Reports
* Forecasted Expense Reports
* Forecasted Tax Reports
* Forecasted Net Income Reports
* Historical versus Forecast Comparison Reports
* Forecast Trend Visualizations
* Forecast Filtering by Client, Period, and Year

In this way, the objective highlights both the forecasting functionality and the forecasting contribution of the study while remaining aligned with the actual implementation of the system.
