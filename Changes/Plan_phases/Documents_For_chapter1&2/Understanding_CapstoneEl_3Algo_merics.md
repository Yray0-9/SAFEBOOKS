## 📊 4. What are Metrics?

Metrics are:

Numbers that measure how accurate your prediction is compared to real data.

Simple meaning:

“How wrong or how correct your forecast is”

Your metrics:

✔ MAE (Mean Absolute Error)  
average error size

✔ MAPE (Mean Absolute Percentage Error)  
error in percentage form

What metrics do:

They answer:

“Which algorithm is most accurate?”

Simple example:

| Algorithm | Error |
|-----------|-------|
| A | 100 |
| B | 50 |
| C | 20 |

👉 C is best because lowest error = highest accuracy

## 🏆 5. What your panel means by “highest accuracy wins”

They mean:

After testing 3 forecasting algorithms, the one with the lowest error (MAE or MAPE) will be selected as your final forecasting model.

NOT guessing  
NOT preference  
BUT based on numbers
