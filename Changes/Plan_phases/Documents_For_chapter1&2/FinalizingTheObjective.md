## Objectives of the Study

The primary objective of this study is to develop **SAFEBOOKS: A WEB-BASED FINANCIAL RECORDS AND COMPLIANCE MONITORING SYSTEM WITH FORECASTING ANALYTICS** that centralizes client financial record management, supports compliance monitoring and client remarks tracking, and improves bookkeeping operations through organized recordkeeping, descriptive analytics, and forecasting outputs.

Specifically, the study aims to achieve this through the following Input, Process, and Output (IPO) phases:

### 1. Input Phase (Requirements and Data Gathering)
To identify and compile the necessary system inputs and data, specifically:
- Compile historical client financial records, including transaction entry details for sales, expenses, and tax-related items.
- Gather compliance tracking parameters, filing deadlines, client activity statuses, and bookkeeper follow-up rules.
- Collect functional requirements and workflows of bookkeeping professionals to design a fitted workspace.

### 2. Process Phase (Analysis, Design, and Implementation)
To process the gathered inputs and execute the system development stages, specifically:
- Compare Moving Average, Weighted Moving Average, and Linear Regression forecasting models using historical client financial records, and evaluate their performance based on Mean Absolute Error (MAE), Mean Absolute Percentage Error (MAPE), and Root Mean Square Error (RMSE) to select the optimal model.
- Design and develop a web-based workspace for managing client business profiles, TIN-based search, and related bookkeeping records.
- Implement structured period-based financial record entry for manually encoded sales, expenses, tax-related items, deadlines, and notes.
- Implement client remarks and compliance monitoring to track client activity status (new, active, separated, closed) and compliance filing status (filed, pending, late).
- Generate financial analytics that summarize recorded financial data, present trends, and integrate the evaluated Weighted Moving Average forecasting algorithm according to monthly, quarterly, or annual transaction schedules.

### 3. Output Phase (Integration and Evaluation)
To deliver and evaluate the system's final components, specifically:
- Integrate dashboard and report outputs that consolidate financial summaries, compliance status, and forecasting results for bookkeeper review and follow-up.
- Evaluate the completed system in terms of usability, performance, and functional correctness in supporting bookkeeping operations.
