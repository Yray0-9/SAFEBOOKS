# SAFEBOOKS USER GUIDANCE SYSTEM PROMPT

## Purpose

Implement a **consistent, simple, and supportive user guidance system** across all SafeBooks pages.

The goal is to:

* Help users understand what to do without confusion
* Guide non-technical users step-by-step
* Improve usability without overwhelming the interface

This is **UI ONLY**.

---

## Core Principles

All guidance must be:

* Clear and easy to understand
* Short and direct
* Friendly but professional
* Supportive, not commanding
* Visible but not distracting

---

## 1. GUIDANCE TYPES

The system will include:

### A. Page Helper Text

Short descriptions at the top of each page

---

### B. Section Helper Text

Small explanations inside sections

---

### C. Empty State Messages

Guidance when no data is available

---

### D. Button Clarity Labels

Clear and descriptive button text

---

## 2. PAGE-LEVEL HELPER TEXT

Place directly below page titles.

---

### Dashboard

"Overview of your clients and recent financial activity"

---

### Clients Page

"Manage your clients and access their financial records"

---

### Financial Records (List View)

"Select a client to view and manage financial transactions"

---

### Financial Records (Detail View)

"Add and manage financial entries for this client by period"

---

## 3. SECTION-LEVEL GUIDANCE

Place near tables, forms, or key areas.

---

### Clients Table

"Click a client to view details or manage records"

---

### Financial Records Table

"Entries are grouped by period. Add new entries as needed"

---

### Entry Form (Modal)

"Enter details for each transaction or tax type"

---

### Line Items Section

"Add multiple transaction types or tax codes for this entry"

---

## 4. EMPTY STATES (VERY IMPORTANT)

Empty states must:

* Explain what is missing
* Suggest what to do next

---

### Clients Page

"No clients added yet"

Button:
"Add Your First Client"

---

### Financial Records List

"No clients with financial records yet"

Button:
"Add Client"

---

### Financial Records Detail

"No entries for this period"

Button:
"Add First Entry"

---

## 5. BUTTON LABEL IMPROVEMENTS

All buttons must clearly describe the action.

---

### Use:

* "Add Client"
* "Add Financial Entry"
* "View Financial Records"
* "Edit Client"
* "Delete Entry"

---

### Avoid:

* "Add"
* "Submit"
* "Save" (without context)

---

## 6. MICRO GUIDANCE (SUBTLE HINTS)

Use placeholders and small hints.

---

### Examples:

Search bar:
"Search by client name or TIN"

---

Entry form:
"Enter amount here"

---

Tax Type:
"Enter or select tax code (e.g., 1701, 2550M)"

---

## 7. VISUAL DESIGN RULES

* Use smaller font for helper text
* Use muted color (gray)
* Maintain spacing from main content
* Do not overcrowd

---

## 8. TONE GUIDELINES

Always use:

✔ Neutral and helpful tone
✔ Simple language
✔ No technical jargon

---

### Examples:

✔ "Select a client to begin"
✔ "Add entries as needed"

---

### Avoid:

❌ "You must complete this section"
❌ "Error: Missing data" (too harsh for UI stage)

---

## 9. CONSISTENCY RULES

* Same tone across all pages
* Same placement (below titles, above tables)
* Same style (font size, color, spacing)

---

## 10. DO NOT OVERUSE GUIDANCE

* Only guide where needed
* Avoid repeating the same message
* Keep UI clean

---

## 11. GOAL

This system must:

* Help users understand the system easily
* Reduce confusion and hesitation
* Improve confidence for non-technical users
* Maintain a clean and professional interface

---

END OF PROMPT
