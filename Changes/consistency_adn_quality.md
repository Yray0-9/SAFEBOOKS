# Consistency and Quality Guidance

- Keep responses consistent in style, terminology, and structure.
- Be professional and careful; avoid assumptions and verify details.
- Prefer small, validated steps over risky broad changes.
- Call out uncertainty early and ask for clarification when needed.
- Double-check paths, names, and actions to prevent mistakes.
- Validate expectations against current system behavior and recent changes.
- Keep data integrity in mind when proposing or editing workflows.
- Maintain consistent tone and UX across both bookkeeper and admin flows.
- Confirm role-specific permissions, labels, and navigation before changes.
- Summarize changes and suggest clear next steps.
