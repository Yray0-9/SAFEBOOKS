# SAFEBOOKS ANALYTICS PAGE UI PROMPT

## Purpose

Create a simple and user-friendly **Analytics Page** that provides meaningful financial insights for bookkeepers.

This page must:

* Support decision-making
* Be easy to understand
* Avoid complexity
* Align with non-technical users

This is UI ONLY.

---

## CORE PRINCIPLE

Show insights, not complexity

---

## PAGE HEADER

Title:
"Analytics"

Helper Text:
"View simple financial insights and trends across your clients"

---

## 1. CLIENT SELECTION (TOP SECTION)

Dropdown or selector:

* Select Client
* Option: "All Clients"

Helper Text:
"Select a client to view financial insights"

---

## 2. FINANCIAL SUMMARY CARDS

Display simple summary cards:

* Total Sales
* Total Expenses
* Total Tax
* Net Value (optional)

---

## Design:

* Same style as dashboard cards
* Clean and minimal

---

## 3. MONTHLY TREND (SIMPLE CHART)

Display:

* Sales over time
* Expenses over time

---

## Design Rules:

* Use simple line chart
* Max 2 lines only
* Do NOT overcrowd

---

Helper Text:
"Shows monthly financial trends based on recorded data"

---

## 4. RISK INSIGHT SECTION

Display:

* Risk Level (Low / Medium / High)
* Short explanation

---

Example:

"High Risk
This client has missing or inconsistent entries"

---

---

## 5. FORECASTING SECTION

Display simple projection:

* "Expected Trend: Increasing / Decreasing / Stable"

---

Optional:

* Small chart (light style)

---

Helper Text:
"Based on previous financial patterns"

---

---

## 6. CLIENT COMPARISON (OPTIONAL SIMPLE TABLE)

Only if "All Clients" selected:

Show:

* Client Name
* Total Sales
* Risk Level

---

Keep:

* Minimal columns
* Easy to scan

---

---

## 7. EMPTY STATE

If no data:

"No financial data available yet"

Button:
"Add Financial Records"

---

---

## 8. DESIGN RULES

* Keep layout clean and spaced
* Avoid too many charts
* Use soft colors
* Maintain blue theme
* Keep everything readable

---

---

## 9. USER GUIDANCE

Include:

* "Select a client to begin"
* "Data updates based on recorded entries"

---

---

## 10. DO NOT INCLUDE

* Complex analytics
* Technical financial ratios
* Too many graphs
* Confusing data

---

---

## GOAL

This page must:

* Help users understand financial performance
* Support simple decision-making
* Feel easy and not overwhelming
* Align with SafeBooks purpose

---

END OF PROMPT
